# The Ever-Unfolding Saga — Roadmap

This is the authoritative overview of unfinished work, current priorities, and planned milestones. It should be possible to understand what remains without searching the source tree or relying on open GitHub issues.

Completed work in the current development line belongs in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); earlier completed work belongs in [CHANGELOG-HISTORY.md](CHANGELOG-HISTORY.md). Detailed design decisions live in [docs/design/](docs/design/README.md), while [system documentation](docs/systems/) describes current implementation behavior and boundaries.

## Cross-cutting release operations

The CI/CD workflow supports artifact-only publication of an existing tag to GitHub Releases, itch.io, or both. It reuses the exact desktop and Android workflow artifacts produced by the tag-triggered build runs, verifies the complete eight-file set, and does not rebuild or re-sign. This manual promotion is available only while the original workflow artifacts remain within their seven-day retention period.

## Current development line — v0.53.x: world topology and spatial-world expansion

**Repository version at this roadmap revision: 0.53.37.**

**Goal:** extend the local spatial prototype into a persistent world whose connected locations and regions have coherent, deterministic foundations.

**Visual realization status:** new generic outdoor maps now project each location's persisted macro-geography footprint into local terrain. Ocean, inland lakes, and the new local river channel bands become impassable water; coast/desert samples become sand, and alpine/strong mountain-ridge samples become rocky rubble; other land remains open floor. River channels are continuous deterministic curves derived from shared watershed links and appear as provisional tinted water tiles. New route plans retain exact sub-sample river-bank intersections and now also retain stable lake identity and exact entry/exit shoreline points for land-to-land ferry crossings. Water and sand still reuse the floor texture with material tints. The local-map generator version advances from 9 to 11 across v0.53.26-v0.53.27; established maps remain unchanged. New generic outdoor maps place a deterministic walkable ford or bridge at each distinct persisted route-bank midpoint projected into the local footprint when a complete bank-to-bank span fits, including multiple crossings on one connected river component. Components without a route target retain one generic fallback span; targets selecting the same span share it, and intersecting spans retain both runs. Narrow/medium channels use fords and wide/very-wide channels use bridges. New route exits use the persisted macro path's actual intersection with the aspect-corrected location footprint, and destination arrivals use their reciprocal passage or an inbound projected edge. Water-safe corridors preserve waterways and refuse false connections without an existing crossing. Route infrastructure now has persistent world-owned records for roads, trails, fords, bridges, ferries, and mountain passes, with independent construction, condition, access, and optional ownership. Built bridges and ferry services require explicit available records; natural fords and passes may remain unowned and usable. Physical passage travel, World Map reachability, and known-route fast travel share the same availability checks. Infrastructure record semantics and route availability enforcement are implemented. Player-facing construction/material costs, maintenance/repair, ownership transfer, and permission resolution are explicitly deferred to v0.55.x so they can reuse the shared resource/inventory, work, relationship, and authority foundations instead of introducing parallel systems. Initial elevation/ridge relief shading, smoothly interpolated shoreline influence, gradual shallow-water/coastal-land accents, sparse biome-aware vegetation, and rocky ridge details now appear as bounded presentation-only overlays. Finished vegetation and mountain silhouettes, dedicated water/terrain assets, and underground geology remain unfinished. Physical use of geography-backed routes now also plays a short transient terrain panorama from up to 16 ordered macro-path samples, distinguishing water, coast, desert, forest, tundra, open country, relative valleys, and mountain terrain with colored placeholders. Existing sky/time presentation, player and actor sprites, material palette, and provisional route/cave-connection tints remain. See [macro-geography](docs/systems/macro-geography.md) for the implemented-versus-rendered boundary and remaining visual work.

**Current local-map scale baseline:** newly generated outdoor maps now default to 64×48 tiles (up from 24×16), while compact interiors remain smaller where appropriate (the authored shrine chamber uses 32×24). World Lab offers 64×48, 80×56, and 96×64 presets. Existing saved maps are never resized. This is an initial increase, not the final scale target: outdoor areas should take several minutes to cross, locations may span multiple local areas, and regions must be much larger than one local map. See [world scale and travel](docs/design/world-scale-and-travel.md).

**Added in this iteration:** underground geology now has an independent deterministic material sampler for pale granite, veil slate, and cinder basalt. It varies across broad, regional, and fine spatial scales and gently with depth; each family exposes relative hardness and fracture descriptors. It applies to underground coordinates regardless of cave topology, remains renderer-neutral and derived on demand, and introduces no save or geography-ruleset change. Dedicated underground material artwork and visual layering remain future work.

**Added in this iteration:** the canonical world clock now advances continuously during active local exploration at one in-game minute per five real-world seconds, including while the player stands still. It pauses with menus, overlays, combat, route transit, and outside the local map. The minute remainder is saved, action and route costs remain additive, and the sky readout/celestial position reflect minute-level progression. Route travel duration continues to derive from the persisted route plan instead of applying a fixed cost to every hop: the estimate separates normalized polyline distance from planner elevation/ridge cost, samples up to 16 route environments for biome difficulty, and adds explicit delays for mountain passes, fords, and ferries. Physical passage travel and known-route fast travel use the same estimate, and the World Map displays estimated journey duration.

**Added in this iteration:** local surface samples now carry elevation, mountain-ridge strength, moisture, and shoreline adjacency into presentation. Continuous fields are bilinearly interpolated across the bounded macro sample grid, while biome, water, mountain-barrier, river-width, and directional shoreline classifications remain nearest-sample data. The spatial renderer uses these fields for smoother relief shading, gradual shallow-water/coastal-land treatment, sparse deterministic vegetation, and rocky ridge details. Surface details are non-colliding, capped per map, and never persisted; no save schema, geography ruleset, or local-map generator version changed.

Targets selecting the same span share it, and intersecting spans retain both runs; components without route targets retain one generic fallback crossing. Added coverage for multiple targets, target-order independence, duplicate targets, and preserving intervening blocked river tiles. The local-map generator version advances from 12 to 13; established maps remain unchanged, with no save-format or geography-ruleset bump. Bumped Rust and Android metadata to 0.53.37.

The authored anchor takes precedence over legacy side-only preferences, resolves deterministically to the nearest valid tile on that side, and applies only to new passages; established passage IDs and positions remain unchanged. Added base-campaign examples, content validation and backward-compatibility coverage, and edge-endpoint placement tests. Bumped Rust and Android metadata to 0.53.36.

**Delivered in v0.53.35:** added an active local-exploration world clock at one in-game minute per five real-world seconds. Time advances while moving or idle, pauses outside active campaign exploration and during menus/overlays/combat/route transit, and persists the minute remainder without a save-format migration. Existing action and route time costs remain additive through the shared two-hour advancement boundary. ## Following milestone — v0.54.x: world content, simulation, character lives, and action combat

**Goal:** make the expanded world more coherent and reactive, and let individual character lives create lasting, comprehensible consequences.

- Build deterministic, context-aware procedural content generation for regional and location identity, landmarks, resources, inhabitants, factions, discoveries, quests, and opportunities. Authored content and mods retain precedence; locations need identity, not mandatory loot or encounters.
- Extend mod support with schema-validated, mod-authored procedural templates and archetypes, stable-ID-based additions/overrides, and clear precedence with authored campaign content. Defer arbitrary scripted hooks until a concrete design justifies their complexity.
- Strengthen the authoritative world-time and simulation contract. Support detailed, event-driven, aggregate, and deferred updates according to gameplay relevance, with explicit state ownership, stable ordering, persistence, and duplicate prevention.
- Add lightweight, time-driven regional climate evolution: seasonal wet/dry patterns, rainfall variation, drought, and structured consequences for settlement water availability, crops/vegetation, and travel. Let rivers and lakes respond to broad wet/dry periods without implementing physical discharge calibration or continuous water simulation.
- Develop the geography-backed World/Region Map presentation from authoritative macro-geography, location footprints, and discovered routes, preserving survey/discovery visibility and existing travel authority. Keep the interim graph fallback where geography or presentation coverage is incomplete; track the work in [#548](https://github.com/Golden-Bastion/the-ever-unfolding-saga/issues/548).
- Extend character progression into meaningful choices, optional backgrounds and ambitions, permanent life records, retirement into persistent NPCs, and continuity rules separating world state, recorded knowledge, and individual knowledge.
- Design and introduce real-time spatial action combat as a deliberate replacement path for the current turn-based encounter model. Establish attack phases, Strong/Fast/Group styles, target handling, assisted positioning, combos, block/parry, injury consequences, structured combat events, and desktop/Android controls. Develop bespoke combat HUD elements and combat-specific art alongside this replacement system rather than polishing the transitional encounter UI in advance.
- Profile representative desktop and Android workloads before deciding simulation limits or presentation budgets. Do not impose arbitrary ceilings on future simulation depth.

Design references: [procedural content generation](docs/design/procedural-content-generation.md), [world simulation](docs/design/world-simulation.md), [character lives and inheritance](docs/design/character-lives-and-inheritance.md), and [real-time spatial combat](docs/design/real-time-spatial-combat.md).

## Later milestone — v0.55.x: social power, livelihoods, and command

**Goal:** turn relationships, resources, and preparation into practical ways to act on and influence the persistent world.

- Establish target-specific loyalty and trust as independent relationship axes; distinguish voluntary commitment, contractual cooperation, fear-induced compliance, coercion, and force.
- Add persistent agreements, recruitment, assignments, group membership, leadership, shared resources, and organizations that can outlive a character.
- Build a small connected livelihood loop using shared resources and inventory across gathering, productive work, crafting, alchemy, and trade.
- Implement route-infrastructure construction, material costs, maintenance/repair, ownership transfer, and permission resolution on top of those shared resource, work, relationship, agreement, and settlement/organization authority foundations. Preserve the independence of construction, condition, optional ownership, and access; do not infer ownership from construction or grant access from ownership alone.
- Make preparation meaningful through equipment, consumables, information, allies, and supplies without imposing a mandatory continuous-needs-management loop.
- Extend spatial combat toward small-group tactical command, then larger group/organization behavior only when the shared systems support it.

Design references: [social influence and organizations](docs/design/social-influence-and-organizations.md) and [livelihoods, preparation, and command](docs/design/livelihoods-preparation-and-command.md).

## Dependencies and sequencing

- World topology/geography is the foundation for frontier expansion and location-aware procedural content.
- Procedural content builds on stable region/location identities and should materialize content idempotently rather than rebuilding the world.
- World simulation provides the time/update/persistence contract used by character lives, social systems, livelihoods, and world evolution.
- Character lives and inheritance depend on correct world-vs-character ownership and structured persistent consequences.
